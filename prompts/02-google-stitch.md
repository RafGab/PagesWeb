# Prompt 02 — Diseño en Google Stitch

Pega esto en https://stitch.withgoogle.com (modo web/desktop y luego pide la versión móvil).

```
Design a modern, high-converting website homepage for a local business.

Business: [NOMBRE], a [SECTOR] in [CIUDAD].
Language of all text: Spanish.
Target customer: [ej. familias de 30-55 años que buscan un dentista de confianza cerca de casa].
Main goal of the page: get the visitor to [ACCIÓN: book an appointment / call / WhatsApp / request a quote].
Brand feel: [ej. limpio, cálido, profesional]. Brand colors: [HEX o "elige una paleta confiable"].

Sections in this order:
1. Sticky header: logo, 4 menu links, highlighted CTA button "[TEXTO CTA]".
2. Hero: strong headline focused on the customer's result, subheadline,
   primary CTA + secondary WhatsApp button, trust badges (Google rating [NOTA] ★, [AÑOS] años, [DATO]).
3. Services grid: [SERVICIO 1], [SERVICIO 2], [SERVICIO 3], [SERVICIO 4] with icon and 1-line benefit.
4. Why us: 3 differentiators with numbers.
5. Before/after or gallery.
6. Google reviews (3 testimonial cards with stars).
7. Process in 3 steps.
8. FAQ (5 questions).
9. Final CTA band + contact form (name, phone, message).
10. Footer with address, hours, map, phone, social links.

Style: generous white space, rounded corners, large readable typography,
real-photo placeholders (not illustrations), floating WhatsApp button, mobile-first.
```

Después: exporta el HTML / captura y úsalo como referencia en el prompt 03.
